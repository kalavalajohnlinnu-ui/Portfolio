About Me

A 17-year-old Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at JNTU K Kakinada building practical, real-world software.

Hi, I'm John Linnu Kalavala, currently studying Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) at JNTU College of Engineering Kakinada (JNTUK), Andhra Pradesh. I fuse fundamental circuit analysis and embedded systems logic with high-performance web engineering, responsive user interfaces, and business workflow automation.

My portfolio spans commercial systems used daily across Andhra Pradesh — including the Thomas International Music Academy (TIMA) studio management & slot booking PWA, KAPL Enterprises automated quotation engines & 6-document batch legal generators, Bharat Pro Lifts engineering matrices, and conference digital ticketing platforms.

Every system I engineer is built for speed, reliability, and human-centric clarity, with zero unnecessary bloat and immediate business value.
